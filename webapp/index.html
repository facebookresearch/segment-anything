The flow

Three moves. Zero friction.

The whole pipeline — the heavy image encoder and the mask decoder — runs locally. Your photo never leaves the tab.

STEP 01

Drop an image

Pick any photo. SAM encodes it on your GPU in a couple of seconds — the embedding is computed once and reused for every hover.

STEP 02

Hover & scrub

Move the cursor to pick the object under it. Scroll or press ↑/↓ to grow or shrink the selection — from a whole person down to their shoelace.

STEP 03

Freeze & remove

Click (or press F) to lock the mask, then Delete to cut it out. Undo, reset, or export a clean PNG with the object gone.

Why it feels different

Studio-grade segmentation, no studio required.

Truly on-device

The encoder runs in a WebGPU Web Worker; the decoder responds to your cursor in milliseconds. Private by construction — your images stay on your machine.

Real-time picking

No "submit" button. The mask tracks your cursor as you explore the scene.

Biggest → smallest

Each point yields nested masks. Scrub the scale to grab the whole car, a wheel, or the valve cap.

Keyboard-first

F freeze · Del remove · ⌘Z undo · ↑↓ scale.

Clean export

Remove an object and download a transparent PNG, or keep stacking removals with full undo history. Great for quick comps, dataset cleanup, and "what would this look like without…".

Under the hood

One model, split in two — and both halves live in the browser.

SAM separates a heavy image encoder from a featherweight decoder. We run a distilled encoder on WebGPU so the embedding is computed once per image; every cursor move only re-runs the tiny decoder. That's why picking feels instant.

Encoder · once

Distilled ViT runs on your GPU, turning the image into a reusable embedding.

Decoder · per hover

Milliseconds per prompt. Your cursor is the prompt; the mask is the answer.

Zero round-trips

No backend, no API key, no quota. Works offline once the model is cached.
